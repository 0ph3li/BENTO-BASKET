# BENTO & BASKET

**便當與菜籃 · four thousand snacks you missed from home.**

*a family minimarket in New York's Chinatown, since 1988. Auntie Mei recommends.*

`HTML` · `CSS` · `vanilla JS` · `GSAP` · `Lenis` · `SVG` · `Web Audio` · `New York time`

</div>

---

**BENTO & BASKET** is a concept website for a shop that doesn't exist: a tiny Asian minimarket at 88 Lantern Lane, a crooked little street off Canal, run since 1988 by **Auntie Mei** and **Uncle Bao**, guarded by **Mochi**, a calico who sleeps on the rice bags. This is its website: aisles, a recipe box, the weekly flyer, the family album and the shop front, plus a basket that follows you everywhere.

The whole site is built like the shop's own printed matter: **flat poster colours, prices in giant red numbers, starbursts, die-cut product stickers and three alphabets on one page**. Every page is a different object you'd find on the counter (a sale poster, a newspaper ad, a recipe tin, a flyer, a photo album, the shop front itself) and gives you something to *do*.

## ✦ The pages

### 00 · Loader
A red curtain with the shop's name counts to **100** while the shop gets ready: *stocking the shelves, waking up Uncle Bao, feeding Mochi, counting the change… open!* Then it lifts like a shutter.

### 01 · Home: *the sale poster*
- **The poster**: BENTO & BASKET around a green shopping basket full of snacks. **Drag them out** and throw them around; *put them back, Mei is watching* resets the basket.
- **Today's prices**: a ticker of the day's prices running under the poster.
- **Uncle Bao's beef noodle soup**: pinned to the scroll in three steps: *grab the goods → into the bowl → 開動! let's eat*. The ingredients fly into the bowl and the dish appears, with a receipt to **shop all 8 ingredients** in one tap.
- **This week's flyer**: SALE, 感謝祭!!!, six deals on starbursts and a countdown to Sunday 8 PM.
- **The Basket Times**: the aisles as a full-page newspaper ad, with products that follow your cursor.
- **Meet Mei & Bao**: after dark, under the 梅寶商店 neon. Polaroids, a speech bubble, and the house rules (*do not wake up Mochi*).
- **Find us**: a receipt with the opening hours and the shop's status *right now*.

### 02 · Aisles: *the store directory*
- **Ask Bao**: type anything and Bao answers, slowly, where it is (*kimchi? aisle 4, Mei's own*), or why he doesn't have it (*no beer. only Horoyoi.*).
- **The store map**: a top-down plan drawn in SVG, with a *you are here*, Mei at the counter and Mochi asleep on the rice. It follows you as you scroll and lights up the aisle you're in.
- **Six aisles, 74 products**: Snacks, Noodles & rice, Sauces & spice, Fresh & frozen, Greens, Drinks & tea. Every product stands on a metal shelf with a price tag that flips to show Mei's note, and **flies into your basket** when you tap it.

### 03 · Recipes: *the recipe box*
- **The tin**: index cards poking out of a recipe tin, *Mei & Bao · keep out*. Pull a card to open it. *Who's cooking tonight?* Mei or Bao.
- **Sixteen cards** on a gingham tablecloth, written in two handwritings, with filters (by Mei, by Bao, under 15 minutes, spicy, veggie, sweet), chilli ratings and a green **素 veggie** stamp on the vegetarian ones.
- **Shop it**: every ingredient sticker flies into the basket, one after the other.
- **Cook it**: the card opens and the **finished dish spins in beside it**. Tick the ingredients and the steps; every step with a time is a real **kitchen timer** that dings.

### 04 · Flyer: *感謝祭 SALE*
- **The cover**: 感謝祭!!! and SALE in a yellow-to-orange gradient under a blue sky with drifting clouds, Mei giving a thumbs up, four price starbursts and the week number, all on New York time.
- **Deal of the week**: a bubble tea kit on a red starburst.
- **The whole flyer**: three pages (Fresh 新鮮, Pantry 乾貨, Sweet 甜) of deals with the old price crossed out and the % you save.
- **Coupons**: four coupons to **clip**: *−5% if you bring your own basket* really comes off the total, the others are a free ramune on karaoke Thursday, *pet Mochi the cat* and a Tuesday special (we're closed).
- **Your basket**: everything you tapped anywhere on the site, **printed as a receipt** line by line. Change quantities, see the SALE lines, the coupons and what you saved, and read Mei's comment at the bottom (*so much chili. drink some mugicha.*).

### 05 · Our story: *the family album*
- **Fifty-one years, one counter**: the wedding polaroids, taped.
- **The album**: it scrolls **sideways**, one page per year (1975 to today), while the year rolls on a **till counter**. Every page has something glued in: the wedding lunch, the first index card corrected in red, two suitcases from Taipei to JFK, the receipt of the first customer, the store plan marked *FINAL*, the karaoke neon, a *gone fishing* sign, Mochi's first night.
- **The cast**: *HELLO my name is* badges for Mei, Bao, Mochi, Mrs. Wu (the dumplings) and Bao's cousin (the eggs).
- **The cork board**: leave Mei a note. It's pinned next to the regulars' notes, and **Mei answers**.

### 06 · Find us: *the shop front*
- **The shop**: a shop front drawn in CSS, with a neon sign, a striped awning, a window full of snacks and a door. The **OPEN / CLOSED** sign flips with the real time in New York. **Ring the bell** and Mei answers, differently in the morning, at lunch, at night and on Tuesdays.
- **Bao's map**: Chinatown drawn in SVG, *not to scale, Bao says it's roughly right*. Pick a route (the Canal St subway, Grand St or Columbus Park), with the real MTA line colours, and it **draws itself** with Bao's directions.
- **Hours**: an analog clock on New York time, today highlighted, and the special days (Lunar New Year, Thanksgiving, snow days).
- **Good to know**: price tags for cash, phone, delivery (*Bao's bike has a flat*), accessibility, parking and pets.

## ✦ Everywhere in the shop

- **The basket**: a counter in the header; tap it and the receipt **slides in** from the side on any page, with quantities, sale prices, the coupon and the total.
- **After hours**: when the shop is closed in New York, the whole site **goes dark**, blue-violet with a pink 打烊 *after hours* neon. A switch in the header forces it to day or night.
- **Mochi**: asleep somewhere on every page, in a different spot every visit.

## ✦ How it works

- **One catalog.** Every product, aisle and sale price lives in `js/catalog.js`. The aisles, the flyer, the basket drawer and the receipt all read from it, so a price changes in one place.
- **New York time.** The opening hours, the door sign, the clock, the flyer countdown, the receipt and the after-hours lights all use the time in `America/New_York`, wherever you read from.
- **Die-cut stickers.** About 80 product, dish and story photos were cut out with a small Python script (flood-fill the background from the edges, soften the mask, crop; an ellipse mask for round plates). CSS adds the white die-cut outline. Everything is **WebP**: 51 MB of PNGs became 6 MB.
- **Image slots.** Every photo is a slot with `data-img`. Drop a file with the right name in `img/<PAGE>/` and it appears; until then a labelled sticker shows what goes there.
- **Sound in code.** The bell, the kitchen timers and the karaoke song are synthesised with the **Web Audio API**: no audio files.
- **Still mode.** Add `?still` to any address and all motion is switched off, with every animation already in its final state. Handy for screenshots.

## ✦ Privacy

Nothing leaves the browser. There is no server, no analytics and no tracking. Your basket, the coupons you clipped, the notes you pinned on the cork board and the lights setting are kept on your device in `localStorage` (keys start with `bb-`).

## ✦ Run it locally

There is no build step. Serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 5188
```

then open <http://localhost:5188>.

## ✦ Built with

- Plain **HTML, CSS and JavaScript**: no framework, no build step
- [GSAP 3](https://gsap.com/) + ScrollTrigger · [Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis) for smooth scrolling
- SVG · Web Audio API · `Intl` time zones
- Google Fonts: Dela Gothic One, Bricolage Grotesque, Space Mono, Yellowtail, Noto Sans TC

Designed for desktop and mobile. Respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, with a skip link, visible focus and keyboard access throughout.
